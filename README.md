# Tune Town 🎵

A music playground for kids that runs in the browser. It has two modes: **🧸 Little Musician** for ages 3–4 and **🎹 Big Keyboard** for older kids. Every sound is synthesized live with the Web Audio API, so there are no audio files.

## 🧸 Little Musician (ages 3–4)

No reading or words needed. Everything is shown with pictures, motion and sound.

- Five big white keys, C to G, each with an animal: 🐱 Cat (C), 🐶 Dog (D), 🐘 Elephant (E), 🐸 Frog (F), 🦒 Giraffe (G)
- 🎨 Free play · ⭐ Find it · 🪜 Stairs · 🦜 Copy me · 🎵 Songs (Hot Cross Buns, Mary Had a Little Lamb, Ode to Joy)
- A bouncing 👆 shows which key to press; wrong taps never make a "wrong" sound, and the right key grows as a hint
- Finished activities earn stickers 🦄🌈🍦

## 🎹 Big Keyboard

- 🎹 **Two-octave keyboard** (C4–C6) with rainbow keys. Works with touch (several fingers at once, slide across keys), mouse, or the computer keyboard (Z row = low octave, Q row = high octave)
- 🎺 **9 instruments**: piano, xylophone, guitar, flute, trumpet, saxophone and organ use real recordings (see `sounds/*/CREDITS.txt`); robot and space are synthesized
- 🥁 **Beat maker**: 16-step drum grid with 5 sounds, 12 ready-made beats, and pace controls (Slow / Walk / Run / Zoom, a slider, and − / + buttons)
- ⏺️ **Recorder**: record a tune and play it back (saved in your browser)
- ⭐ **Learn a song**: follow the glowing star to play Twinkle Twinkle, Mary Had a Little Lamb, Ode to Joy, Jingle Bells, Happy Birthday and Old MacDonald
- Key names as C D E, Do Re Mi, or hidden; light and dark themes

Open `index.html` locally, or visit https://net13200.github.io/Music-dashboard/
