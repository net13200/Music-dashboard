👋 For grown-ups: how to play
  • 🎨 Free play: tap any key. Each note has its own animal: C = 🐱 Cat, D = 🐶 Dog, E = 🐘 Elephant, F = 🐸 Frog, G = 🦒 Giraffe.
  • ⭐ Find it: an animal shows up in the bubble. Find its key (the 👆 helps). 5 in a row earns a sticker.
  • 🪜 Stairs: climb up C D E F G and back down, following the 👆.
  • 🦜 Copy me: watch and listen to the animals sing (👀), then copy them (👆). It starts with 1 note and grows to 3.
  • 🎵 Songs: 🥐 Hot Cross Buns, 🐑 Mary Had a Little Lamb and 🎻 Ode to Joy. 👂 plays the song first.
  • Wrong taps still make music, so there's no "wrong" sound. After two misses the right key gently grows as a hint.
  • Pick a sound at the bottom: 🎹 piano, 🪇 xylophone, 🪈 flute or 🤖 robot.
